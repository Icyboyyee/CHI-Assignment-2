EVIDENCE SUPPORTS THIS CONDITION

Chosen and studied, not imposed.

Article 3 of the 1994 agreement mandated selective adoption under Chinese law while respecting PRC sovereignty. Submitting Suzhou’s own 1995 draft to the working committee and sending eight officials for 2002 Singapore training provide concrete behavioral evidence of active selection.

Interpreted through our course framework, this case represents mutual learning in economic governance: within two culturally connected societies organizing state–business relations differently, Suzhou selectively adapted Singaporean investor-service practices (“harmony without uniformity”). Primary records here mainly document changes on the Suzhou side.

About this exhibit

This interactive diagram uses an illustrative firm and simplified routes. The departments shown are schematic. Recorded dates, institutions and the scope of the 2005 example are source-referenced.

Lower coordination costs are an interpretation of the institutional change. The exhibit focuses on local implementation and retains uncertainty about the full comparison of Singaporean and Suzhou approval powers.

Source verification, bilingual localization and interface code were AI-assisted. The exhibit evolved from a local timeline sketch and static dual-track infographic into this three-setting hall comparison, with an English/Chinese toggle for side-by-side checking. Rather than citing a chronicle turnaround figure (59 to 25 days) whose matter scope is unspecified, the exhibit anchors 2005 to the documented US$30m FIE procedure. Records checked November 2026.
